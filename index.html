The reason we’re here

Support should show up before things feel too heavy.

Stress, anxiety and loneliness can be hard to talk about. Even when a campus has counsellors, stigma or hesitation can make that first visit feel far away. MOOD'O'METER imagines a gentle, voluntary bridge from a check-in to a real conversation.

A preventive step, not a replacement for care.

A way to think about support✳

Not every day needs the same thing.

The concept uses three broad indicators to help guide the next step.

Green · feeling okay

Wellness activities and ways to keep looking after yourself.

Yellow · could use support

Self-help resources, with the option to speak to a counsellor.

Red · priority connection

A prompt to connect with an appropriate human support resource.

Colours are not clinical assessments. A trained professional—not an automated score—should guide care.
